# PRODUCT.md

## Register

product

## Product Purpose

Lampcircle ("live song maps") is a local-first web app for leading spontaneous worship in small living-room settings. Import a recorded song, map it into parts (tap the 1s, flag sections, cut and fade, pickups), save the map as .djw, then perform: queue parts on pads so transitions land on the right beat.

## Users

One worship leader (Kevin first). Preps calmly on a laptop beforehand; performs live on a laptop or tablet in a dim room, eyes mostly on people, not the screen.

Flow: Library → Tap → Prep → Perform.

## Brand Personality

Warm, quiet, intimate. Soft lamp, small circle. Unhurried, never flashy.

## Anti-references

- DJ / Ableton density, knobs everywhere.
- Concert-production visuals.
- Housefires is the feeling, not the look.

## Strategic Principles

- Perform: readable at a glance in a dim room; safe from mid-song mistakes (wrong pad, accidental stop).
- Prep feels like a track editor (cut, snap, fade), not a panel of looper settings.
- Import works; keep it. Auto-analyze is out of scope.
- Local-first: songs and maps stay on the device.

## Accessibility

Keyboard-first (Space, section keys). Large touch targets on Perform. Every control has a visible label or accessible name.
